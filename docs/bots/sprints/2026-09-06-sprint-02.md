## Incident

An accidental `git checkout -- .` by the lead reverted unstaged tracked-file
edits mid-sprint (the lead's dependency bumps, redone; the senior developer
was told to re-verify their tracked edits). Lesson: never discard with a
working-tree-wide checkout while agents are editing; use `git stash` scoped to
paths or a worktree for trial runs.
